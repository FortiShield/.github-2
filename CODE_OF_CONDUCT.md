## Safe and Respectful Contributions

- We prohibit contributions or discussions containing violent, abusive, or unsafe content.
- Any code or content designed to cause unauthorized harm, damage, or violate laws is strictly forbidden.
- Contributors must comply with [GitHub’s Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).
- Violators may be blocked or reported to GitHub.

Let's keep our community safe and constructive.
